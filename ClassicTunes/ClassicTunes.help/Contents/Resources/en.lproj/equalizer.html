Equalizer

Open the Equalizer

Choose View > Show Equalizer (⌥⌘E). It opens the Equalizer tab of Settings.

Use it

  1. Turn on Enable Equalizer.
  2. Pick a Preset, or drag any of the ten sliders (32 Hz to 16 kHz, −12 to +12 dB).
  3. Moving a slider switches the preset to Custom.
  4. Click Reset to return all bands to flat.
