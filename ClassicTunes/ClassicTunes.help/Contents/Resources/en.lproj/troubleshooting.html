Troubleshooting

My songs don’t appear

Check that a music folder is chosen in Settings > General > Library, then click Rescan Library Now.

Lyrics are missing

Lyrics need to be in the song’s metadata or found online. Check your internet connection, or paste lyrics in Song Info > Lyrics. If there are no lyrics in the metadata and are not available through a service called lrclib, it will not work.

My iPod doesn’t show up

Only 1st and 2nd generation iPod Shuffle models are supported. Make sure the iPod is in disk mode and mounted in Finder. Try reconnecting with the app open.

A menu command is dimmed

Most commands apply to the main window. Click the ClassicTunes window to bring it into focus.

I want to start over

Use Settings > General > Data & Reset to clear play history or reset appearance.
