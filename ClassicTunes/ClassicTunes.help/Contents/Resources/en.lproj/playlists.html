Playlists

Create a playlist

Choose File > New Playlist… (⌘N) or click New Playlist at the bottom of the Playlists section. Enter a name and click Create.

Add songs

  • Drag a song from the list onto a playlist in the sidebar.
  • Or Control-click a song, choose Add to Playlist, and pick a playlist.

Playlist artwork

Click the artwork at the top of a playlist and choose Change Photo… to set your own image, or Remove Custom Photo to go back to the default.

Import and export M3U

Use File > Import Playlist (M3U)… (⇧⌘O) and File > Export Playlist (M3U)… (⇧⌘E), or the Import M3U / Export M3U buttons in the bottom bar.

Delete a playlist

Select it and choose Edit > Delete Playlist (⌫). Your song files are not deleted.

Built-in playlists

ClassicTunes builds some playlists automatically from your library, including Recently Played, Top 25 Most Played, Recently Added, 90s Music and Classical Music. They update as you listen and cannot be edited. Use Settings > General > Data & Reset > Clear Play History… to empty play counts and play history.
