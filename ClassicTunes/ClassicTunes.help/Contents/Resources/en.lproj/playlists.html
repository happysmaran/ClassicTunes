Playlists

Create a playlist

Choose File > New Playlist… (⌘N) or click New Playlist at the bottom of the Playlists section. Enter a name and click Create.

Smart playlists

A smart playlist fills itself with songs that match rules you choose, and updates as your library changes. Choose File > New Smart Playlist… (⌥⌘N), name it, and set up the rules:

  • Choose whether songs must match all or any of the rules, then click Add Rule for each condition.
  • Each rule tests a field (Title, Artist, Album, Album Artist, Genre, Composer, Grouping, Comment, Year, Plays, BPM, Track Number, Disc Number or Duration) with an operator such as contains, starts with, is greater than or is in the range. Rules left blank are ignored.
  • Turn on Limit to to cap the playlist at a number of songs, chosen by random, most or least played, title, artist, album, or newest or oldest year.

To change the rules later, Control-click the smart playlist in the sidebar and choose Edit Smart Playlist…. You can export a smart playlist to M3U like any other playlist.

Add songs

  • Drag a song from the list onto a playlist in the sidebar.
  • Or Control-click a song, choose Add to Playlist, and pick a playlist.
  • Songs can’t be added to smart playlists by hand; they follow their rules.

Playlist artwork

Click the artwork at the top of a playlist and choose Change Photo… to set your own image, or Remove Custom Photo to go back to the default.

Import and export M3U

Use File > Import Playlist (M3U)… (⇧⌘O) and File > Export Playlist (M3U)… (⇧⌘E), or the Import M3U / Export M3U buttons in the bottom bar.

Delete a playlist

Select it and choose Edit > Delete Playlist (⌫). Your song files are not deleted.

Built-in playlists

ClassicTunes builds some playlists automatically from your library, including Recently Played, Top 25 Most Played, Recently Added, 90s Music and Classical Music. They update as you listen and cannot be edited. Use Settings > General > Data & Reset > Clear Play History… to empty play counts and play history.
