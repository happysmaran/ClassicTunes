Settings

Open Settings with ClassicTunes > Settings… (⌘,).

General

  • Appearance – System, Light or Dark.
  • Theme Color – accent colour for selections and controls.
  • Library – choose or rescan your music folder.
  • Playback – resume on launch, pause on output change, crossfade.
  • Data & Reset – reveal playlists in Finder, clear play history, reset all appearance settings.

Backgrounds

Set the Album Grid and Song List backgrounds to a colour or a custom image. In Dark appearance the album grid always uses Dark.
