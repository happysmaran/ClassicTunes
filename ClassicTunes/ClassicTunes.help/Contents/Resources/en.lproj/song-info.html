Editing song info

Open Song Info

Select a song and choose File > Song Info… (⌘I), or Control-click it and choose Song Info….

Tabs

  • Summary – file details such as kind, size, bit rate, sample rate, channels, date modified, plays and last played.
  • Info – name, artist, album, album artist, genre, year, track and disc numbers, composer, grouping, BPM, comments and the compilation flag.
  • Lyrics – view or edit lyrics.
  • Artwork – drag in an image, or click Choose Artwork…. Remove Artwork clears it.

Click OK to save or Cancel to discard.

ClassicTunes will not be able to save information to certain file formats, and instead are saved inside the appfolder itself. ClassicTunes will notify you when that is the case.
