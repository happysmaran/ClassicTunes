Syncing an iPod Shuffle

ClassicTunes can, at the moment, sync to 1st and 2nd generation iPod Shuffle devices. More devices are planned in the future.

Sync songs

  1. Connect the iPod. It appears under Devices in the sidebar; select it.
  2. Click Add Songs… in the view. In the picker, choose songs and click Add to Sync List.
  3. Click Sync and confirm. ClassicTunes writes the iPod’s database and audio files.

Syncing replaces all songs currently on the iPod with the sync list. This behavior is intended to be replaced with better syncing logic in the future.

Eject

Wait for Sync complete, then click Eject iPod and confirm. Ejecting during a sync can corrupt the device.

Remove a song from the sync list

Use the remove button beside the song before you sync.
