Browsing your library

The sidebar

Library holds your Music. Store opens the Tunes Store. Playlists lists built-in and your own playlists. A connected iPod appears under Devices.

Change the view

ViewMenuShortcut
ListView > as List⌘1
Albums (grid)View > as Albums⌘2
Cover FlowView > as Cover Flow⌘3

You can also use the three view buttons on the right side of the toolbar.

Search

Press ⌘F (Edit > Find) to jump to the search field, then type to filter songs.

Song list columns

Control-click (or right-click) the column header to show or hide columns such as Artist, Album, Genre, Time, Track, Disc, Year, Composer, Plays and Comment. Choose Reset Columns to restore the defaults.

Song context menu

Control-click a song for Play Next, Add to Playlist, Find Music Video and Song Info….
