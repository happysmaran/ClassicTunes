ClassicTunes Help

ClassicTunes is a music player that recreates the look and feel of iTunes 7–10. Choose a topic to get started.

  • Getting started – import your music folder and play a song
  • Browsing your library – List, Albums and Cover Flow; search; the sidebar
  • Playlists – create playlists, smart playlists, import and export M3U, built-in playlists
  • Playback controls – shuffle, repeat, Up Next, lyrics, crossfade, visualizer, MiniPlayer
  • Equalizer – presets and ten-band control
  • Editing song info – tags, artwork and lyrics
  • Tunes Store – search music, movies, TV shows and books; preview songs
  • Syncing an iPod Shuffle – add songs, sync and eject
  • Settings – appearance, theme, backgrounds, library, data
  • Keyboard shortcuts – default shortcuts and how to change them
  • Troubleshooting
