Playback controls

Play a song

Click on a song to start playing it. Use the playback buttons at the top (previous, play/pause, stop, next) and the volume slider to control playback. Keyboard controls also work.

Shuffle and repeat

Click the shuffle or repeat button in the toolbar, or use the Controls menu. Repeat cycles through Repeat All, Repeat One and off (⌘R). Shuffle is ⌘S.

Volume

⌘↑ and ⌘↓ change the volume; ⇧⌘↓ mutes or unmutes.

Up Next

Choose View > Show Up Next (⌘U) or click Up Next in the bottom bar to see the queue. Use Play Next from a song’s context menu to queue it.

Lyrics

Choose View > Show Lyrics (⌘L) or click Lyrics in the bottom bar. Lyrics come from the song’s metadata, or are looked up online from lrclib when none are embedded. You can also edit them in Song Info.

Crossfade

In Settings > General > Playback, turn on Crossfade songs and set an overlap from 1 to 12 seconds.

Pause when the output changes

Turn on Pause when the audio output device changes in the same section to stop playback automatically when headphones are unplugged or you switch speakers.

MiniPlayer

Choose View > Switch to MiniPlayer (⇧⌘M), or Control-click the player display and choose Open in MiniPlayer. The MiniPlayer shows the current song, playback buttons and a Next Up view. Press ⇧⌘M again, or its close button, to return to the full window.
