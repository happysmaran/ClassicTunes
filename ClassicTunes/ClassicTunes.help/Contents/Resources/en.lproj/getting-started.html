Getting started

Import your music

ClassicTunes requires an accessible folder with music files.
  1. Choose File > Import Music Folder… (⌘O), or click Import Music in the Settings Menu (⌘,).
  2. Select the folder that contains your music files. ClassicTunes scans it and fills Music under Library.

To change the folder later, or to pick up new files, open Settings > General > Library and click Change… or Rescan Library Now.

Play a song

Click on a song to start playing it. Use the playback buttons at the top (previous, play/pause, stop, next) and the volume slider to control playback. Keyboard controls also work.

Resume where you left off

Turn on Resume last song on launch in Settings > General > Playback.

Movies, TV Shows, Podcasts and Radio appear in the sidebar but are currently unavailable. They are planned with a future ClassicTunes release.
