Tunes Store

Search

Select Tunes Store in the sidebar, choose a media type (All, Music, Movies or TV Shows), then type in the search field.

Preview and view

Click Preview to hear a clip, and Stop to end it. View in Store opens the item’s page outside the app.

The store requires an internet connection. ClassicTunes searches and previews only; it cannot purchase or download items, and the Buy button opens actual iTunes or Apple Music.
