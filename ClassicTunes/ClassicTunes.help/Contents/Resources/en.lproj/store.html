Tunes Store

Search

Select Tunes Store in the sidebar, choose a media type (Music, Movies, TV Shows, Books or All), then type in the search field and press Return. Changing the media type re-runs your search.

Results

Results appear as a grid of cards with artwork, title, artist and price. Items without a price show Free.

Preview and view

Click the play button on a song or music video card to hear a clip, and click it again to stop. Previews play through the main player. Click the price button to open the item’s page outside the app.

The store requires an internet connection. ClassicTunes searches and previews only; it cannot purchase or download items, and the Buy button opens actual iTunes or Apple Music.
